# Brand guidance for the selected UI

These are concise, source-backed notes for Thoughtloom's selected Quiet Night designs, **not a replacement authoritative personal-brand guide**. Source revision and exact copied resources are recorded in [SOURCE.md](SOURCE.md).

- Use the full [16-color functional palette](colors.toml) and sensible foreground/background mixtures. The four-color identity mark does not impose a four-color ceiling on application UI.
- Quiet Night's selected ground is Night `#031315`; reading/input fields use dark mixtures, not light panes. [ui-palette.json](ui-palette.json) records the selected roles and rounded sRGB mixtures.
- Parchment `#FEF6E1` is the brightest neutral; Weathered Ivory `#D5D3C5` supports readable body text. Avoid pure white as a new theme color.
- Use Lantern for the primary action, Horizon for selection/focus, and the functional palette for meaningful links, attribution, and feedback. There is no requirement to display all 16 colors simultaneously or give every material kind a different hue.
- Color reinforces explicit wording. User/AI byline colors do not mean accepted/rejected, true/false, or user endorsement. Future feedback states need clear labels and symbols, not color alone.
- Use the bundled [Source Sans 3](fonts/SourceSans3-VariableFont_wght.ttf) for working headings, body text, navigation, and controls. Preserve its [OFL license](fonts/OFL.txt).
- Keep controls familiar, borders restrained, and hierarchy precise. Brand expression serves reading and operating the product; it is not a terminal costume or decorative landscape.
- Write plainly, distinguish evidence from interpretation, and respect user agency. Reading an AI contribution is not agreement.

No identity artwork is bundled or altered. The PNG comps are visual references, not an implemented accessible component system; real contrast, keyboard/focus, and responsive behavior require their own review.
