# Shared navigation

Keep shared navigation basic, with three buttons:

- **Explore:** opens the [Search / Explore view](search-view.md). This is the navigation label for search, not a separate agent mode.
- **Capture:** opens the [Capture view](capture-view.md).
- **Settings:** reserved as a shared destination; its contents and behavior remain undefined. Do not invent settings to fill it.

The mockups' additional Ideas and Journal destinations, notification bell, and profile control are not requirements. Idea and Evolve remain contextual views reached through the flows described in their notes, not additional shared-navigation buttons.
